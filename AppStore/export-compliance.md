# Export compliance — account-holder worksheet

The app uses encryption for HTTPS/TLS connections and AES-GCM protection of local voice profiles.
The repository does not establish the final App Store export-compliance answer.

Before submission, the account holder must determine under Apple's current questionnaire and
applicable law whether the use is limited to exempt standard encryption, whether a French import
declaration applies, and whether documentation such as a US classification/report is required.
Record the determination, reviewer, date, jurisdictions, and supporting documents here. Do not set
`ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` merely to bypass the questionnaire; add it only when the verified
answer for the exact shipped binary is known.
