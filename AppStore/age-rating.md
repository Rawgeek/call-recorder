# Age rating — draft questionnaire

Expected result: the lowest general-audience rating, subject to the current App Store Connect
questionnaire and account-holder confirmation.

Suggested answers for the app's supplied content and features:

- Violence, sexual content/nudity, profanity/crude humor, horror/fear, alcohol/tobacco/drugs,
  gambling/contests, guns/weapons: None.
- Medical/treatment information: None.
- Unrestricted web access: No.
- User-generated content or messaging hosted/shared by the developer: No.
- Advertising: No.
- Location sharing: No.

The app can record user-chosen real-world conversations, whose content the developer does not
provide or moderate. The account holder must answer using Apple's current definitions and should
not treat this draft as a legal or platform-policy determination.
